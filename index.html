PEACE OF MIND GUARANTEE

How Your Gear is Protected

Trust is the core pillar of MitrKosh. We protect both lenders and borrowers.

🔒 Automated Security Deposit Escrow

Every borrowing request is backed by a security deposit held in automated escrow. Funds are never released to the lender unless damage occurs, and are refunded directly to the borrower upon inspection.

📋 2-Minute Handover Condition Checklist

When handing over an item, both parties quickly review standard condition checklist items (accessories, power state, cosmetics) on their phone. Both sign off digitally with a verification code.

⚡ Instant Deposit Return

Once the item is handed back in good condition and the lender confirms the return, the security deposit is automatically credited back to your account within 60 seconds.
